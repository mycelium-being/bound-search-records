# Ledger: what was tried, with which idea

One row per try. The row is the sentence tried, not the thinker: a thinker's work makes many claims in many ways, and a run scores exactly one of them, as translated by whoever wrote the row. So each row names the claim and its place in the text, the translation into one wiring, what the wiring removed or added, the prediction written before the run, the result, and whose the loss is. A loss belongs to the **translation** when the wiring names a structure the stock model learns on its own (so the run could only reproduce a known result), and to the **claim** only when the wiring is a faithful carrying of the sentence and still loses. "Open" means neither has been shown. A row is written before its run, with the prediction, and the result is filled in after; a row never changes its claim or its prediction once the run has started.

The judge for rows 1 to 3 is the one fixed on the page: best validation loss on shakespeare_char over 5000 iterations at the stock nanoGPT config, mean of two seeds, against the unmodified baseline at matched parameters, with a compute-matched baseline added where the variant costs more per step. Over 0.01 on both seeds is a result; under 0.01 is a tie. From row 4 the judge changes in two ways, after an outside reading on 2026-11-15 pointed out that the 0.01 band had been fixed with no relation to seed spread (the baseline's two seeds differ by 0.0005 at best val, Peirce's by 0.0117): three seeds per arm, and the tie band set from the baseline's own three-seed spread by a formula written into the row before any arm runs. The `kind` column names what the wiring did to the stock model in one word: **fixed** (a learned score replaced by a given function), **removed** (a learned structure taken away, nothing in its place), **recomputed** (a learned score replaced by a quantity the model computes about itself), **constituted** (a learned parameter replaced by a record of the model's own states).

## Standing

Four claims run, eleven runs, no win. Three losses: Kant's and Hume's the translation's, in each the wiring was a relation the stock model learns unaided; Wittgenstein's the claim's by the rule written before the run, with the mechanism measured afterwards pointing at one choice inside the translation (a zero-initialised record of use is a fixed point of its own update, so the model never received token identity). One tie, Peirce, open. The pattern so far, which is a hypothesis and not a result: a claim that adds or removes a relation loses to the model that learns the relation; a claim that changes what a score is computed from has not lost; a claim that replaces a learned table with a record of use has lost once, for a reason the next row can remove. Row 4b is written above with its prediction, the same band and kill line, and runs next; its result column is empty until the hand's file exists.
